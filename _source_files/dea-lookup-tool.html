How It Works

Simple to use. Impossible to get wrong.

Designed for pharmacists and technicians — not IT staff. Up and running in minutes.

01

Enter or upload

Paste a single DEA number or upload a CSV export from your pharmacy system. No reformatting required.

⚡
02

Live query executes

Skypond queries the DEA registrant database in real time and retrieves the current registrant record instantly.

Who It's For

Real scenarios where DEA verification matters

From daily dispense workflows to quarterly audits — verification needs come up constantly in LTC pharmacy.

Pre-dispense verification at the point of fill

Build DEA lookup into your CII dispense workflow so technicians confirm prescriber authorization before every controlled substance fill — not after a compliance question arises.

→ Verification at the point of dispensing

New prescriber onboarding

When a facility sends over a new attending physician or NP, verify their DEA number instantly — before their first controlled substance order reaches your queue.

→ Zero delay on new prescriber activation

Periodic compliance audits

Run your entire active prescriber list through bulk lookup monthly to surface any expired or revoked registrations before they create a dispensing violation or inspection issue.

→ Proactive compliance, not reactive cleanup

DEA inspection preparation

Pull a complete verification history for any prescriber or time period to demonstrate due-diligence practices. Every lookup logged with user, timestamp, and result — ready in minutes.

→ Inspection-ready documentation always on hand
