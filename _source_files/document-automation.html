How It Works

From template to signed archive — automatically

Most document types are live within a week of kick-off. Your team sees the time savings from the first document generated.

📄
01

Build template library

Work with our team to build templates for your most common document types — PA letters, facility agreements, consent forms, compliance notices.

🔌
02

Connect data sources

Templates link to your pharmacy system so patient, medication, prescriber, and facility fields populate automatically from existing records.

⚡
03

Trigger and generate

A technician or pharmacist triggers a document from the relevant workflow. The populated document is ready for review in seconds.

✅
04

Route, sign, archive

The document routes through your configured approval workflow. Once fully signed, it is automatically archived with a complete audit trail.

Who It's For

The document workflows that drain time most in LTC pharmacy

These are the scenarios where manual document handling causes the most friction — and where automation pays off fastest.

Prior authorization letters

Generate prior auth requests with complete clinical and prescription data pre-populated. Send directly to payers without manual transcription — and track status without hunting through email.

→ PA letters in seconds, not 20 minutes

Facility service agreements

Auto-generate and route new facility service agreements for pharmacist review and facility administrator e-signature when onboarding new accounts — no back-and-forth email required.

→ Onboarding paperwork in hours, not days

Patient consent and admission forms

Generate HIPAA-compliant consent forms for new admissions pre-populated with patient and facility data. Route to facility for collection and archive automatically — no paper forms to track down.

→ Admission paperwork handled without paper

DEA and compliance communications

Auto-generate prescriber notifications for expired DEA registrations, refill limit notices, and controlled substance compliance communications — without drafting from scratch each time.

→ Compliance notices out in one click
