Bill to the right payor — before the claim goes out

Payor source changes are the most common reason LTC pharmacy claims get rejected. A Medicare spell ends, a resident moves to managed care, a private-pay patient qualifies for Medicaid — if your system doesn't know, the claim goes out wrong.

The PCC feed ensures your billing team sees every payor change in time to correct it before submission, not after a denial.

  • Medicare, Medicaid, managed care, and private pay tracked
  • Billing team notified before the next claim cycle
  • Fewer rejections from stale or incorrect payor data

Who It's For

Built for real LTC pharmacy scenarios

Whether you serve 5 facilities or 150, the operational problem is the same — and the feed solves it at any scale.

Billing to the right payor, every time

Payor source changes are the most common cause of LTC pharmacy claim rejections. The feed delivers those changes before your next claim cycle — not after a denial that requires rework and resubmission.

→ Fewer rejections, faster reimbursement

Eliminating the manual PCC report run

Many pharmacies have staff running manual census and payor reports from PCC multiple times daily. The feed replaces that entirely. Data arrives automatically — your staff focuses on pharmacy work instead.

→ Hours recovered every week, per facility

Relying on facilities to provide and maintain PCC credentials creates operational risk — when logins expire or change, your workflow breaks. With the feed, you never need facility-level access to stay current.
