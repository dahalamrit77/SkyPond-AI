How It Works

From opening count to always-current inventory

Setup is fast. Once live, the system runs continuously with no daily maintenance from your team.

📋
01

Configure formulary & locations

Set up your CS formulary and storage locations. Initialize inventory from your opening count or a data import.

📝
02

Record transactions as they happen

Technicians log receives, dispenses, returns, and waste in real time via the interface or integrated dispensing system.

🚨
03

Discrepancies surface automatically

The system continuously compares recorded transactions against counts. Any deviation outside tolerance triggers an immediate alert.

✅
04

Resolve and document

Staff investigate and document resolutions. The full chain — what happened, who reviewed it, what action was taken — is stored permanently.

Manage perpetual inventory across hundreds of CS SKUs and thousands of daily transactions without a clipboard or spreadsheet in sight. Discrepancies surface before the shift ends.

→ Real-time accuracy at any dispense volume

Facility-based satellite locations
